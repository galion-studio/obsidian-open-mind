   are overwritten in pass 2 of 02:xx. Fix exists: swarm/nerve-dst-clock c229a875fb5ea1028b9eb27fb9f11efb185eeebf
   (names keep `+0200`/`+0100`, retention by instant). Not deployed. A restart alone does not help: the loss is
   independent of the cadence phase (pass 2 = pass 1 + 3600 s, cadence 600 s divides 3600).
2. **Human log reading in 02:xx (medium):** `journalctl --since` defaults to the host zone (UTC), which is the
   safe choice; giving it a Zagreb wall time from 02:00-02:59 on 2026-10-25 is ambiguous. Use UTC or a stamp
   with an explicit offset.
3. **Backup at 02:17 +02:00 falls inside the no-change window (low):** it is automatic and fold-safe (above);
   do not run a manual `galion-backup`/restore test in the window, so no archive is made in pass 2 of 02:xx.
4. **Offset-less legacy names (low):** `/var/backups/galion/nerve/galion-nerve-YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS.bin`,
   `cutover-YYYYMMDD`, `repo-YYYYMMDD-HHMM` dirs. Safe as long as nothing is deployed in the window.
5. **Clock (low):** no NTP inside the LXC container; time comes from the OVH host. Skew today <= 2.3 s. If the
   host clock drifted, stamps would drift with it; nothing in the switch depends on NTP.
6. **Timer local times move by one hour (info):** galion-backup becomes 01:17/07:17/13:17/19:17 +01:00. Any doc
   or dashboard that states "backup at 02:17" in Zagreb time is off by one hour after the switch.
