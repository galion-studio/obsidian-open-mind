The short version

  • Your Galion account is a random 16-digit number. We do not ask for your name, email or phone.
  • We do not store your IP address. We keep no activity logs about what you do with your account.
  • We keep only what we need to know whether your account has paid time, and the totals our accountant is required by law to keep.
  • If you pay by card, Stripe handles the payment. Stripe may ask for your email and country and keeps its own records. If you want no link at all between you and your account, pay by cash (or by crypto, when it is switched on).
  • If you run Galion yourself (the Free, open source version), it runs on your computer and sends nothing to us.

Your numbered account

When you press “Generate account number”, our server creates a random 16-digit number and shows it to you once. That number is your user name and your password.

  • We store only a scrambled form of the number (a one-way hash made with a secret key). It cannot be turned back into the number. This is why we cannot show you your number again, and we cannot recover a lost number.
  • With the account we store: the plan (Free, Plus or Pro) and the date your paid time ends.
  • Anyone can create as many accounts as they like. An account number does not tell us who you are.

Payments

Card (Stripe)

Card payments run on Stripe’s own checkout page. We never see your card number.

  • We send Stripe a random payment reference. We never send it your account number or an email.
  • Stripe may ask you for an email address and your billing country (for receipts, fraud checks and VAT) and keeps its own records as a payment company. For that processing Stripe is generally its own controller; see Stripe’s privacy policy.
  • Be aware: while a monthly card subscription is active, we keep Stripe’s customer and subscription ids next to your account. Someone holding both our records and Stripe’s could link that account to the card holder. When the subscription ends, we delete those ids.

Cash by post

You get a reference such as GC-PA20-FS10-VCT8. Put only that reference on a slip of paper, with euro notes, in an envelope, and post it to [CASH POSTAL ADDRESS]. Please do not write your name, your address or your account number on the envelope or the slip.

We open the envelope, add the time to the account, and shred the envelope and slip. We keep the day, the amount and the currency in the money ledger, with no link to any account.

Why we are allowed to process this

  • To provide the service you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): account, sessions, plan and paid time, payment references, Stripe ids.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): the money ledger for accounting and tax.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): rate limits and security, to protect accounts from guessing and the service from abuse.

We make no automated decisions about you, and we do no profiling.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete personal data about you, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable form.

Because we do not know who owns an account, in most cases we hold nothing that identifies you (GDPR Art. 11). If you can show us your account number, we can tell you what is stored for that account, and delete the account. Deleting an account cannot be undone and ends any paid time.

You can complain to the Croatian data protection authority, Agencija za zaštitu osobnih podataka (AZOP), [AZOP ADDRESS], azop.hr, or to the authority where you live.

If authorities ask

We follow the law. But we can only hand over what we have, and that is described above: for most accounts, “some account has paid time until a certain date”, with no way to say who owns it.
