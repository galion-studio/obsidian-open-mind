How it works

Light enough for any computer. Fast on all of them.

Galion is made to do a lot with a little. No giant servers, no special hardware: the computers you already have are enough.

Built in Rust

Rust is a programming language known for speed and safety. Galion is written in it from top to bottom, so it is small, quick and sturdy.

Runs on an ordinary processor

Galion is built for the everyday chip in your computer. If one of your machines has a graphics card, Galion will use it too, but it never needs one.

Yours, even offline

Connect any laptop or device and it serves you at home, on your own network, even without the internet. To answer, it uses an AI model you choose: one on your own computer, or an online one.

Private by design

No email. No name. Just a number.

You do not need an email address or any personal details. We give you an account number, and that number is your account. Keep it somewhere safe: it is the only key to your account.
