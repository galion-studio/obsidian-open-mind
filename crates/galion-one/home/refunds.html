Refunds and the 14-day right of withdrawal

Last updated: [DATE OF PUBLICATION]

The short version

  • If you live in the EU, you can withdraw from a new Plus or Pro subscription within 14 days, without giving a reason.
  • Because the hosted service starts right away, we ask you at checkout to agree to that. If you withdraw within the 14 days, we refund what you paid [in full / minus the days already used].
  • After 14 days, you can cancel any time and keep the time you have already paid for. We do not refund part-months, except where the law requires it or we close the service.
  • The Free version costs nothing, so there is nothing to refund.

[Decide one: (A) full refund within 14 days, like Mullvad’s money-back guarantee; or (B) the legal minimum, a refund minus a fair amount for the days already used.]

Starting right away (consent at checkout)

Paid plans begin as soon as your payment reaches us. At checkout you will see:

“I want my Galion subscription to start right away. I understand that if I withdraw within 14 days, [I get a full refund / I pay for the days I have already used].”

[To confirm the wording under Directive 2011/83/EU (Art. 14(3) and 16(a), as amended by 2019/2161) and the Croatian Consumer Protection Act. Also decide how we confirm the contract and this consent on a durable medium without an email, for example a printable receipt on the account page.]

How to withdraw or ask for a refund

Tell us clearly, within 14 days of your first payment for a plan, that you want to withdraw. You can use the form below, but you do not have to. Send it to [CONTACT EMAIL OR OTHER CONTACT CHANNEL] or by post to [ADDRESS].

To find your payment we need one of:

  • Card: the payment reference shown on your account page, or the Stripe receipt (date and amount). You do not have to give us your account number.
  • Cash: your account number and the cash reference (GC-…), plus how you would like the money back (see below).
  • Crypto (when available): your account number and the payment reference.

We will confirm that we received your request and refund within 14 days of receiving it.

How the money comes back

Paid byRefund goes to
Card (Stripe)The same card, through Stripe. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
Cash by post[Decide: bank transfer to an IBAN you give us, or cash by post to an address you give us.] Either way you would be telling us who you are, so we delete those details once the refund is done, apart from what accounting law requires.
Crypto[The same coin, to an address you give us, for the euro amount you paid. Decide who pays the network fee.]

The paid time on the account ends when the refund is made.

After 14 days

  • Card: cancel any time on your account page. The subscription stops at the end of the month you have paid for. No further charges.
  • Cash and crypto: these are one-off top-ups that never renew. Unused time is not refunded, except as below.
  • If the service did not work as promised, contact us. You have legal remedies for digital services (fixing the problem, a price reduction or ending the contract), whatever you paid with.
  • If we close your account without a good reason under the Terms, or stop the hosted service, we refund the unused paid time.

Examples

  • You subscribe to Plus by card on 1 March and withdraw on 10 March: [Option A] €20 back to your card. [Option B] €20 minus 9/30 of €20, so €14 back.
  • You subscribe on 1 March, keep it, and cancel on 20 April: no refund. Plus keeps working until the end of the month you paid for, then the account goes back to Free.
