What Open Mind is

Memory, tools and agents, working together.

Open Mind is the stack that makes your computer think with you. It remembers what matters, it reaches for the right tool, and it lets small agents split up the work. All of it on your own hardware.

Memory

It remembers your projects, your notes and how you like things done, so you never start from zero.

Tools

Files, the web, your apps and your own scripts. Open Mind picks the right tool and shows you what it used.

Agents

Small helpers that take on parts of a task and report back to your agent, so big jobs get done in parallel.

Private

It runs on your own hardware. Your data stays with you unless you choose to share it.

Lightning fast

Built in Rust, light on memory and quick to answer, even on a small board like the Raspberry Pi.

Free and MIT

Free to use, free to change, free to share. Open source under the MIT License.

Your agent at the centre

No menus. Just your agent.

Your own AI agent is the centre of Open Mind. It does the work in front of you, where you can watch every step, and it always asks before anything important. It can even write its own code, and its own compilers, on top of Galion OS.
