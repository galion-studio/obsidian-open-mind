Galion OS

Galion Operating System

Galion OS is a free, open-source operating system built on Linux and Rust. It runs on any device, from a laptop to a tiny Raspberry Pi. MIT licensed.

One window. One gradient. One prompt field. Type or speak, then watch what happens.

Ask Hermes anything…

A preview of the prompt field. Today it opens Hermes in Galion One.

Hermes at the centre

Hermes is your own AI agent. There are no menus. You say what you want, and Hermes does the work in front of you, step by step, where you can see it. It always asks before anything important.

It writes its own code

Hermes writes its own programs on top of Galion OS, and even its own compilers. When it needs a tool that does not exist yet, it can build one, and you can read every line.

The Obsidian Open Mind stack inside

Memory, tools and agents working together. Open Mind remembers what matters to you, gives Hermes the tools it needs, and lets small agents share the work. Meet Open Mind.

Runs on any device

Laptops, desktops and ARM boards, including Snapdragon machines and the Raspberry Pi. Built in Rust to stay light and fast on ordinary hardware.

Private by default

Your data stays on your own hardware. Nothing leaves the device unless you say so.

Galion OS is the direction we're building towards, starting with Open Mind and Hermes. Downloads come once the first image is ready.
