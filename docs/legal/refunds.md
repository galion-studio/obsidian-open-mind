# Refunds and the 14-day right of withdrawal

Last updated: [DATE OF PUBLICATION]

## The short version

- If you live in the EU, you can withdraw from a new Plus or Pro subscription within **14 days**,
  without giving a reason.
- Because the hosted service starts right away, we ask you at checkout to agree to that. If you
  withdraw within the 14 days, we refund what you paid, minus [the days you already used, or:
  nothing, see the decision below].
- After 14 days, you can cancel any time and keep the time you have already paid for. We do not
  refund part-months, except where the law requires it or we close the service.
- The Free version costs nothing, so there is nothing to refund.

## Starting right away (consent at checkout)

Paid plans begin as soon as your payment reaches us. At checkout you will see:

> "I want my Galion subscription to start right away. I understand that if I withdraw within 14
> days, [I get a full refund / I pay for the days I have already used]."

[LAWYER: confirm the wording. Under the EU Consumer Rights Directive (2011/83/EU, Art. 14(3) and
16(a), as amended by 2019/2161) and the Croatian Consumer Protection Act (Zakon o zaštiti
potrošača), a service that starts in the withdrawal period can still be withdrawn from, with a
proportionate payment for what was provided, unless it has been fully performed. A monthly
subscription is not fully performed within 14 days. We also need to send confirmation of the
contract and this consent on a durable medium: decide how, given that we have no email (for
example a downloadable or printable receipt on the account page).]

## How to withdraw or ask for a refund

Tell us clearly, within 14 days of your first payment for a plan, that you want to withdraw. You
can use the form below, but you do not have to. Send it to [CONTACT EMAIL OR OTHER CONTACT
CHANNEL] or by post to [ADDRESS].

To find your payment we need **one** of:

- **Card:** the payment reference shown on your account page, or the Stripe receipt (date and
  amount). You do not have to give us your account number.
- **Cash:** your account number and the cash reference (`GC-…`), plus how you would like the
  money back (see below).
- **Crypto (when available):** your account number and the payment reference.

We will confirm we received your request and refund within **14 days** of receiving it.

The paid time on the account ends when the refund is made. Withdrawing does not cost you anything
extra, apart from [any proportionate amount under option B].

## After 14 days

- **Card:** cancel any time on your account page. The subscription stops at the end of the month
  you have paid for. No further charges.
- **Cash and crypto:** these are one-off top-ups that never renew. Unused time is not refunded,
  except as below.
- If the service did not work as promised, contact us. You have legal remedies for digital
  services (fixing the problem, a price reduction or ending the contract), whatever you paid with.
- If we close your account without a good reason under the [Terms](/terms), or stop the hosted
  service, we refund the unused paid time.

## Examples

- You subscribe to Plus by card on 1 March and withdraw on 10 March: [Option A] €20 back to your
  card. [Option B] €20 minus 9/30 of €20 = €14 back.
- You subscribe on 1 March, keep it, and cancel on 20 April: no refund, Plus works until the end of
  the month you paid for, then the account goes back to Free.
